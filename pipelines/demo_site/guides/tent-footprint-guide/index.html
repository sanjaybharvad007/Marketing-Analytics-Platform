Do you need a tent footprint?

A footprint is a groundsheet cut slightly smaller than your tent floor. It protects the floor from abrasion and sharp stones, and it adds between 150 and 300 grams to your pack.

Use one on rocky or thorny ground. Skip it on soft forest duff or when every gram counts.
