Sleeping bags

Temperature ratings follow the ISO 23537 standard, which reports a comfort rating and a lower limit for each bag. Choose a bag by its comfort rating if you sleep cold, and remember that the rating assumes an insulated pad underneath you. Down is lighter and packs smaller for the same warmth, while synthetic fill keeps insulating when it gets damp. Fill power describes how much space a gram of down occupies; higher numbers mean more loft for less weight.

A sleeping pad's R-value adds directly to your warmth, and an R-value of four or more is sensible for shoulder seasons. Store bags loose in a large cotton sack rather than compressed, so the insulation keeps its loft for years. A draft collar and a full-length zipper baffle make a larger difference on cold nights than an extra fifty grams of fill.
